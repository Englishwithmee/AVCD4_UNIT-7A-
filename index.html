03 / GRAMMAR · P.124

Already or yet?

Study the rules, then complete three book exercises. Click to choose your answers.

Present perfect

Use the present perfect to talk about something that has or has not happened up to now. In this lesson, we ask whether an activity is complete.

S + have / has + past participle (V3)
ALREADY · BEFORE NOW

Use already mainly in affirmative sentences. An action has happened, sometimes earlier than expected.

S + have/has + already + V3

I've already been there.
She has already left the office.

YET · UP TO NOW

Use yet in negative sentences and questions. It usually goes at the end.

S + haven't/hasn't + V3 + yet.
Have/Has + S + V3 + yet?

I haven't been there yet.
Have you been to the new café yet?

SubjectAffirmativeNegativeQuestion
I / you / we / theyhave already eatenhaven't eaten yetHave … eaten yet?
he / she / ithas already eatenhasn't eaten yetHas … eaten yet?

Short answers: Yes, I have. / No, I haven't. / Not yet.

Questions with already: Have you already finished your science project? The speaker is surprised that the project is finished so soon.

In American English, already and yet can also be used with the simple past. Exercise 3 uses “I already sent you a wedding invitation.” The present perfect “I've already sent …” is also correct.

Check past participles and word order

be: been · go: gone · eat → eaten · see → seen · speak → spoken · have → had · send → sent · do → done · pack → packed · start → started

She has already left.   She hasn't left yet.
Choose have or has to match the subject. Use the past participle after have/has.

STEP 3 / 45–60 SECONDS

My city experiences

Talk about things you have and have not done in Ho Chi Minh City or your own city. Use at least two sentences with already, one sentence with not … yet and three city features words.

I live in …
I've already …
I've also …
I haven't … yet.
I'd like to … next.
Show a model talk

I live in Ho Chi Minh City. I've already walked along Nguyen Hue Walking Street, and I've already taken photos of the fountain there. I've also crossed a bridge over the Saigon River and seen the city at night. There are many apartment buildings, streetlights and busy intersections. I haven't explored every part of the city yet, but I'd like to visit more places with my friends. Next, I'd like to spend an afternoon by the river and take more photos.
